## The case
One day, twelve frozen attacks on headline anomalies: the NANOGrav background, IceCube hotspots, DESI evolving dark energy, the SH0ES ladder, GRB selection, CHIME repeaters, dark-matter direct detection, the neutron lifetime, the proton radius, JWST massive galaxies, strong-lens Hubble tension, muon g-2. Two ran to completion. Nine hit the same wall: the decisive public data product does not exist.

## How it died
Item by item, the missing input is exact and documented: no release-bound joint likelihood exposing the three leave-out axes (NANOGrav); a private likelihood build path behind public chains (DESI); unlabeled full-ladder arrays with no row-identity sidecar (SH0ES); no missed-population or injection product (Fermi GBM); no telescope injection/selection model (CHIME); no detector release with radon/time/sideband/efficiency controls together (dark matter); one beam lineage only (neutron lifetime); no apparatus-lineage labels (proton radius); no joint mass/completeness/lensing release (JWST); no cross-family posterior samples (lenses); no machine-checkable dependency graph (muon g-2). The two that ran: IceCube's hotspot came back ordinary; the CHIME repeater sampling contrast stayed numerically strong but its uncertainty sim was not bit-reproducible.

## What still stands
A verified map from twelve famous anomalies to their exact missing data products, plus one ordinary-sky IceCube result. Ceiling: audit-readiness, no physics claims.

## What would revive it
Each named product, from the people who have them: collaboration likelihood bundles, injection grids, lineage tables, labeled covariance arrays, dependency graphs. Any ONE arriving reopens its route.
